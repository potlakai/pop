Log 02 / Leadership philosophy and style

I assume people want to do good work.

That is Theory Y, and it makes me a democratic leader: guidance without pressure, decisions made in the room.

Theory X

People need control

Not my default. I only get directive when a deadline is close and someone has to call it.

Theory Y · my default

People are self-motivated

Democratic style. I bring a first version, then the group shapes it.

Theory Z

Decide together

Shared goals, consensus, and credit to the individual who did the work. How our exec board runs.

Where it showsOn Raftaar's exec board, money decisions are made as a group. I bring the numbers. The board makes the call.
